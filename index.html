About

Built to build.

T47 Group is a Phoenix-based holding company. We start, operate, and back companies across technology, staffing, and consumer products — bringing capital, systems, and hands-on operating experience to each one.

Our approach is simple: build things that last. Whether it's a software studio, a consumer app, a marketplace, or a workforce business, we treat every venture as a company meant to stand on its own — with real customers, real revenue, and room to grow.

From first idea to launch and beyond, the group shares design, engineering, and operating muscle across the portfolio, so each company moves faster than it could alone.
